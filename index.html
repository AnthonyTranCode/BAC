Monolith

Most years dissolve.

365 days of meetings, errands, and weekends that blur into one another. By December you can't say what happened.

Place the biggest stone first.

Then build the rest of the year around it. The errands, the appointments, the meetings, and the deadlines all arrange themselves once the big one is set.

Finally, choose a monolith. One thing you'll tell people about.

Choose a commitment big enough that no matter what else the year holds, that's the one you'll remember. Then hang it on a wall where you have to look at it.

2027 is 365 days. Here they all are.

One email a quarter. If there's nothing worth saying, we don't send.

One year. One monolith.
